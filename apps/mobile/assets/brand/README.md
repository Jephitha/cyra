# Cyra brand assets

The SVG files are the source of truth. The mark is a continuous asymmetric C-shaped cycle with
a soft gold endpoint, designed to remain legible in one color and at 24 px. `cyra-lockup` is the
primary and horizontal lockup; `cyra-wordmark` and `cyra-mark` support contexts where only one
part is needed. Black and white variants intentionally remove the accent color.

PNG exports use `@1x`, `@2x`, and `@3x` suffixes. For the icon-only mark these correspond to
128, 256, and 384 px; wordmark and lockup exports scale from their SVG view boxes.
