# Screenshot Plan — Cyra

---

## Design Guidelines

- **Color scheme:** Soft pink (#E75480), deep navy (#1A1A2E), white background
- **Typography:** Inter (matching app font)
- **Device frames:** iPhone 15 Pro for iOS, Pixel 8/9 for Android
- **Status bars:** Clean, empty (no time/battery for clarity)
- **Background:** Gradient from white to soft pink (#FFF5F7)
- **Text overlay font:** Inter Bold, white/light pink, drop shadow for readability

## iOS Screenshots (6 total)

### Screenshot 1: Period Calendar — Overview
**Purpose:** Hook — show the core experience immediately.

**What it shows:** The main cycle calendar view in month layout. Current date highlighted with a pink dot. Cycle phases color-coded: period days in pink, fertile window in soft green, ovulation day in bright pink. A small card at the top shows "Period predicted in 3 days" with a countdown.

**Text overlay:** "Track your period & cycle"
**Subtitle:** "Beautiful calendar view with personalized predictions"
**Supporting text:** "Period, fertile window, and ovulation at a glance"

---

### Screenshot 2: Fertility Tracking
**Purpose:** Show fertility features — key differentiator from basic period trackers.

**What it shows:** The fertility tracking dashboard. Split view showing:
- Left: BBT chart with thermal shift line (pre-ovulation low temps, post-ovulation high temps)
- Right: OPK test results timeline (negative = empty circle, positive = pink filled, peak = dark pink star)
Below: Cervical mucus summary for the current cycle day

**Text overlay:** "Fertility tracking, simplified"
**Subtitle:** "BBT, OPKs, and cervical mucus — all in one place"
**Supporting text:** "Know your fertile window with confidence"

### Screenshot 3: Symptom Logger

**Text overlay:** "Log symptoms in seconds"

**Supporting text:** "Discover patterns in how your body changes"

## Screenshot Order Strategy (Maximum Conversion)

### iOS Order (optimized for App Store)
1. Period Calendar (hook — immediately recognizable)
2. Symptom Logger (show ease of use, breadth)
3. Fertility Tracking (differentiator)
4. AI Insights (premium value)
5. Pregnancy Mode (expand audience)
6. Privacy + Data Control (closing — address #1 concern)

### Android Order (optimized for Play Store)
1. Period Calendar (hook)
2. Fertility Dashboard (differentiator)
3. Symptom Logging (breadth of features)
4. Health Reports (practical value — Android users are more likely to share with doctors)
5. Pregnancy Mode (expand audience)
6. Privacy + Community (closing — address privacy + belonging)

## Technical Specifications

### iOS Screenshots
- **Format:** PNG
- **Resolution:** 1290 x 2796 px (iPhone 15 Pro 6.7")
- **Color space:** sRGB
- **Max file size:** 5MB each
- **Localization:** Export as separate layered PSD for each language

### Android Screenshots
- **Format:** PNG
- **Resolution:** 1080 x 2340 px (Pixel 8)
- **Color space:** sRGB  
- **Max file size:** 5MB each
- **Required:** 2 phone screenshots minimum, 8 max (we use 6)
- **7-inch tablet screenshots:** Optional, 600 x 1024 px if pursuing tablet market
