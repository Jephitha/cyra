## 4. Keyword Ranking Strategy

### Phase 1: Launch (Months 1-3)
Focus on low-competition, high-relevance keywords to establish baseline rankings:
- BBT tracker
- cervical mucus tracker
- PCOS symptom tracker
- encrypted health app
- private period calendar

### Phase 2: Growth (Months 3-6)
Scale to medium-competition keywords as app authority grows:
- ovulation tracker
- fertility tracker
- PMS tracker
- period calendar
- cycle tracker

### Phase 3: Scale (Months 6-12)
Target high-volume keywords with brand + keyword strategy:
- period tracker (with "Cyra" prefix)
- pregnancy tracker
- women health app
- natural family planning

### On-Page ASO Tactics
1. **Title** — front-load "Cyra: Period Tracker & Ovulation" (uses top 3 keywords)
2. **Subtitle** — "Private Fertility & Cycle Calendar" (covers privacy angle + cycle keywords)
3. **Description** — 5% keyword density, natural integration, first 3 sentences contain primary keywords
4. **Screenshot text overlays** — include keywords in screenshot callouts
5. **What's New** — always include 1-2 keywords in update notes

### Localization Considerations for Women's Health
- Menstrual taboos vary by culture — adjust marketing tone accordingly
- Some countries require local data storage (GDPR, LGPD, etc.)
- Saudi Arabia, UAE, and other conservative markets require discreet app icon/naming options
- India requires robust data localization and has specific medical app regulations
- EU markets require transparent data processing disclosures
