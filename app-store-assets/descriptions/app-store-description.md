## Long Description (4000 chars max)

Cyra is a holistic women's health tracking app built around one core principle: your data belongs to you. From period tracking to pregnancy, Cyra gives you powerful insights while keeping your health information completely private.

**PRIVACY-FIRST DESIGN**
Cyra encrypts your health data end-to-end. Your cycle, symptoms, and fertility information stays on your device. Even when you choose to sync to the cloud, your data is encrypted before it leaves your phone. No ads, no data selling, no tracking.

**PERIOD TRACKING**
Log your period with ease. Track flow intensity, pain levels, and bleeding type. Cyra learns your cycle patterns and predicts your next period with increasing accuracy over time. View your cycle history with beautiful, intuitive charts.

**OVULATION & FERTILITY TRACKING**
Identify your fertile window with confidence. Cyra combines multiple fertility signs:
- Basal Body Temperature (BBT) tracking with automatic coverline detection
- Ovulation predictor kit (OPK) logging with photo support
- Cervical mucus observations
- Cervical position tracking
- Fertile window predictions based on your unique patterns

**SYMPTOM TRACKING**
Track over 30 pre-defined symptoms across physical, emotional, and lifestyle categories. Log severity levels, add notes, and discover patterns in how your body changes throughout your cycle. Cyra's pattern analysis helps you identify what's normal for you.

**PREGNANCY MODE**
When you're ready to track a pregnancy, Cyra has you covered. Log estimated due dates, track fetal measurements, record appointments, and capture your pregnancy journey with journal entries.

**AI-POWERED INSIGHTS**
Cyra's on-device AI analyzes your cycle data to provide personalized insights:
- Cycle length variability and regularity analysis
- Symptom pattern recognition (PMS patterns, ovulation signs)
- Fertile window accuracy tracking
- Luteal phase assessment
All analysis happens on your device — your data never leaves without your permission.

**HEALTH REPORTS**
Generate comprehensive PDF health reports to share with your healthcare provider. Include cycle summaries, fertility data, symptom logs, and mood tracking in a beautifully formatted document.

**JOURNALING**
Keep a private health journal alongside your tracking. Record your thoughts, moods, and experiences. Your journal entries are encrypted and completely private.

**APPLE HEALTH INTEGRATION**
Sync BBT, heart rate, sleep, and activity data from Apple Health to enrich your tracking without manual entry.

**COMMUNITY**
Connect with a supportive community of women sharing experiences. Posts are moderated to maintain a safe, supportive space. Choose to post anonymously for sensitive topics.

**EDUCATION**
Learn about your body with our library of evidence-based articles covering menstrual health, fertility, pregnancy, hormonal health, and common conditions like PCOS and endometriosis.

**MEDICAL DISCLAIMER**
Cyra is a tracking and informational tool only. It does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or contraceptive advice. Always consult with a qualified healthcare provider regarding your health. Cyra is not FDA-approved for contraception. If you are trying to avoid pregnancy, use an FDA-approved contraceptive method.

**SUBSCRIPTION**
Cyra offers a free tier with essential tracking features. Cyra Premium unlocks AI insights, health reports, advanced analytics, and unlimited history. Subscription options: monthly and yearly.
