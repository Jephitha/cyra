## Full Description (4000 chars max)

Cyra is a holistic women's health tracking app built around one core principle: your data belongs to you. From period tracking to pregnancy, Cyra gives you powerful insights while keeping your health information completely private.

**PRIVACY-FIRST DESIGN**
Cyra encrypts your health data end-to-end. Your cycle, symptoms, and fertility information stays on your device. Even when you choose to sync to the cloud, your data is encrypted before it leaves your phone. No ads, no data selling, no tracking. Period.

**PERIOD TRACKING**
Log your period with ease. Track flow intensity, pain levels, and bleeding type. Cyra learns your cycle patterns and predicts your next period with increasing accuracy over time. View your cycle history with beautiful, intuitive charts and calendar views.

**OVULATION & FERTILITY TRACKING**
Identify your fertile window with confidence. Cyra combines multiple fertility signs:
- Basal Body Temperature (BBT) tracking with thermal shift detection
- Ovulation Predictor Kit (OPK) logging with photo storage
- Cervical mucus consistency, amount, and sensation tracking
- Cervical position, firmness, and openness tracking
- Personalized fertile window predictions

**SYMPTOM TRACKING**
Track over 30 pre-defined symptoms across physical, emotional, lifestyle, digestive, and skin categories. Each symptom can be rated by severity (1-5). Add custom notes and discover your personal patterns over time.

**CYCLE PATTERN ANALYSIS**
Cyra analyzes your logged data to provide meaningful insights:
- Cycle length trends and variability
- Period length consistency
- Symptom patterns by cycle phase
- Ovulation timing accuracy
- Luteal phase assessment
All analysis runs on your device for maximum privacy.

**PREGNANCY TRACKING**
Track your pregnancy journey with dedicated tools:
- Estimated due date tracking
- Fetal growth measurements
- Gestational age calculator
- Pregnancy journal
- Appointment reminders

**HEALTH REPORTS**
Generate comprehensive PDF reports to share with your healthcare provider. Choose from cycle summaries, fertility reports, symptom analysis, or full health reports. Your data, your way.

**JOURNAL**
Keep a private, encrypted health journal alongside your tracking. Tag entries, rate your mood, and look back on your health journey.

**EDUCATIONAL RESOURCES**
Access a growing library of evidence-based articles written by health professionals. Topics include menstrual health basics, fertility awareness, PMS/PMDD, PCOS, endometriosis, and hormonal health.

**COMMUNITY**
Connect, share, and learn from a supportive community. Post anonymously when you need to. All content is moderated to maintain a safe, welcoming space for everyone.

**WEARABLE INTEGRATION**
Connect Apple Health, Google Fit, Oura, Fitbit, Garmin, and Whoop to automatically sync health metrics into your tracking.

**WHY CYRA?**
Most women's health apps sell your data. Cyra was built differently. We believe your most intimate health data should be private by default. No advertisers, no data brokers, no compromises.
